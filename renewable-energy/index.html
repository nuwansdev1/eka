Renewable Energy

Clean power. Lower bills.

We design, install and maintain solar energy systems for homes, businesses, industries and farms.

What we do Talk to our team

Power from the Sun, Built to Last

Sri Lanka enjoys strong sunshine all year round, which makes solar one of the most practical ways to reduce electricity costs and dependence on fossil fuels. EKA Renewable Energy delivers complete solar solutions, from the first site visit to long-term maintenance.

Our engineering and software background means every system is properly designed, safely installed and easy to monitor, so you always know how much energy you are generating and saving.

Why work with us

  • End-to-end service: survey, design, installation and support
  • Systems sized to your actual energy use
  • Remote monitoring of performance and faults
  • Solar pumping and irrigation for EKA Agriculture and other farms

What We Do

Solar solutions for every scale, from a single home to a factory roof.

Residential Rooftop Solar

Grid-connected rooftop systems that reduce or offset household electricity bills.

Commercial & Industrial Solar

Larger rooftop and ground-mounted systems for offices, factories, hotels and warehouses.

Off-Grid & Hybrid Systems

Battery-backed solutions for remote sites and for keeping the power on during outages.

Solar for Agriculture

Solar water pumping and irrigation that cut diesel and grid costs on farms.

Energy Audits & Consulting

A clear look at how you use energy and where you can save, with costed recommendations.

Monitoring & Maintenance

Remote performance monitoring, cleaning, inspections and repairs to keep systems at peak output.
