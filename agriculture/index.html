Agriculture

Growing smarter, from soil to market.

We combine practical farming know-how with modern technology to make agriculture more productive, sustainable and profitable.

What we do Talk to our team

Farming for the Future

Agriculture is at the heart of Sri Lanka’s economy and communities. EKA Agriculture works with growers, landowners and investors to plan, develop and manage productive farmland using sustainable practices.

Because we are part of a technology group, we bring tools that are often out of reach for smaller farms: sensors that track soil and weather conditions, automated irrigation, and simple software to manage crops, inputs and sales.

Why work with us

  • Sustainable, resource-efficient practices
  • Decisions backed by data and technology
  • Support from planning to harvest and sale
  • Solar-powered options with EKA Renewable Energy

What We Do

Services for farmers, landowners and agri-businesses.

Crop Cultivation

Planning and cultivation of fruit, vegetable and plantation crops suited to local soil and climate.

Farm Development & Management

Land preparation, planting plans and day-to-day farm management for landowners and investors.

Smart Farming & IoT

Soil-moisture, weather and crop sensors with dashboards and alerts, built by EKA Software.

Smart Irrigation

Automated, water-saving irrigation systems, including solar-powered water pumping.

Supply Chain & Market Linkage

Connecting farms with buyers, processors and markets so produce reaches customers at a fair price.

Advisory & Training

Practical guidance and training for farmers on modern techniques, crop care and record keeping.
