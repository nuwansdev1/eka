Sri Lanka, Your Way

From ancient cities and sacred temples to misty tea country, wildlife parks and golden beaches, Sri Lanka packs remarkable variety into a small island. EKA Tourism plans journeys around what you want to see and how you like to travel.

We take care of the details, including itineraries, accommodation, transport and guides, so you can relax and enjoy the experience.

Why travel with us

  • Tailor-made itineraries for individuals, families and groups
  • Reliable transport with experienced drivers
  • Carefully selected accommodation for every budget
  • Farm stays and village experiences with EKA Agriculture

Experiences

A few of the journeys we can build for you.

Culture & Heritage

Ancient cities, rock fortresses and temples across the Cultural Triangle and Kandy.

Beaches & Coast

Relaxing stays on the south, east and west coasts, with surfing, whale watching and coastal towns.

Hill Country & Tea Trails

Scenic train rides, tea estates, waterfalls and cool mountain air in the central highlands.

Wildlife & Nature

Safaris in national parks to see elephants, leopards and birdlife in their natural habitats.

Eco & Agri-Tourism

Farm stays and village experiences that connect visitors with sustainable rural life.

Corporate & Group Travel

Business trips, team retreats, events and group tours, planned end to end.
