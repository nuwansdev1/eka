01

Corporate Law

We provide comprehensive corporate and secretarial services to private companies, companies limited by guarantee, non-governmental organisations, and listed and unlisted public companies.

Company secretarial

  • Incorporation of companies
  • Filing of statutory documentation
  • Maintenance of all statutory registers, including share registers
  • Convening Board and shareholder meetings
  • Filing and maintenance of minutes and resolutions
  • Handling official correspondence with the Registrar of Companies
  • Attending to share transfers and share allocations
  • Updating the Registrar of Companies on corporate changes

Corporate advisory & transactions

  • Guidance on Board of Investment (BOI) matters
  • Drafting, reviewing and negotiating commercial contracts
  • Structuring and managing joint ventures, mergers and acquisitions
  • Legal audits and due diligence, including corporate governance
02

Trademark & Patent Law

Registering and protecting your brand and your inventions under Sri Lankan law.

Services

  • Trademark registration in Sri Lanka, including brand names, logos and slogans, under Sri Lankan trademark law
  • Patent registration in Sri Lanka, including drafting, filing and prosecution
03

Conveyancing & Deed Work

Preparing and attesting all types of deeds, carefully and in accordance with the law.

Deeds & documents

  • Last Wills
  • Deeds of Transfer
  • Gifts

 

  • Sale & Purchase Agreements
  • Leases
  • Preparing and attesting all other types of deeds
