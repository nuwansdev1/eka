About Us

EKA Group is a Sri Lankan company founded in 2019 by a group of young, talented individuals who strive to turn business ideas into realities.

Our Group

EKA started with the idea of building software solutions in parallel with research and development, to keep up with the rapidly evolving information technology field. The same approach, understanding the problem first and then building a practical solution, now guides every part of the group.

Today EKA Group operates five departments: Software, Agriculture, Renewable Energy, Tourism and Legal. Each has its own specialist team, and all of them share the group’s technology, experience and network.

  • Technology-led: our software expertise supports smart farming, solar monitoring and better service across the group.
  • Sustainable: we favour solutions that are good for our clients, communities and the environment.
  • Client-focused: clear communication, realistic timelines and long-term relationships.
